    The situation in the Occupied Palestinian Territory also continues to demand the region and the world’s attention.

    Gaza’s recovery must be Palestinian-led, consistent with international law, and preserving of the unity of Gaza and the West Bank, including East Jerusalem.

    Over the past year, the Quintet -- comprising the African Union, the European Union, Intergovernmental Authority on Development, the League of Arab States and the United Nations -- has provided an important framework for strengthening multilateral engagement in support of an inclusive, Sudanese-led civilian dialogue and a future credible political process.

    Looking ahead, the Quintet will continue to work in close coordination to bolster support for Sudanese civilian stakeholders and advance collective efforts to resolve the crisis, laying the groundwork for lasting peace and the stability which Sudan deserves.

    Excellencies,

    This 17th General Meeting of Cooperation between the United Nations and the League of Arab States comes at a time of increasingly constrained resources and diminishing political will to seek durable political solutions through multilateralism.

Closer cooperation and coordinated action serve as force multipliers, enabling both our Organizations to achieve a far greater impact together than either could alone, for the benefit of the people we serve.
